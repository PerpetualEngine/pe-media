# Questions Posed to Dr. Anthony Fauci

**Hearing:** Senate Homeland Security & Governmental Affairs Committee — "Testimony of Anthony Fauci"
**Date:** July 29, 2026 (transcript published July 30, 2026 by Rev)
**Chair:** Sen. Rand Paul · **Ranking Member:** Sen. Gary Peters
**Source:** Rev transcript, "Fauci Congressional Hearing"

> Every question below was answered by Dr. Fauci with the same sentence (a few minor wording variations): *"On the advice of counsel, I respectfully decline to answer based upon my rights under the Fifth Amendment to the Constitution."* The questions are reproduced verbatim; where a senator strung several questions together in one turn, they're kept as delivered.

## FIRST ROUND

### Sen. Rand Paul
1. Why did you choose to fund the research in Wuhan — what was the rationale, the discussion, what went behind the decision? How did you come to the conclusion that a totalitarian country would be capable of doing this in a BSL‑2 lab that lacked the security? And are you sorry for making the decision — given another chance, would you say maybe it wasn't a wise thing? Can you explain to the Committee why you chose to fund this dangerous research in China?
2. In the red folder on your table is a copy of Section 192 of Title 2 of the U.S. Code… Do you have that in front of you?
3. Have you refused this pardon, or do you have any reason to believe this full and unconditional pardon — this grant of immunity — would somehow require you to plead the Fifth Amendment?
4. Do you still refuse to answer the questions?
5. Dr. Fauci, were you ever made aware of gain‑of‑function research on monkeypox being conducted at the National Biodefense Analysis and Countermeasure Center back at Fort Detrick?
6. Dr. Fauci, did the CIA ever transfer funds to NIH under an inter‑agency agreement?
7. Dr. Fauci, did the NIH ever conduct research for or in coordination with any element of the intelligence community — CIA, ODNI?
8. Dr. Fauci, have you been read into any classified Department of Homeland Security program — any prospective special access program concerning gain‑of‑function research?
9. Dr. Fauci, were the scientists at the Wuhan Institute of Virology capable of inserting a furin cleavage site into a coronavirus?
10. Was the Wuhan Institute of Virology combining an S protein from one virus with the backbone of others — and could this lead to a virus that gained function and then could lead to a pandemic?
11. Dr. Fauci, at any of the labs where this dangerous research was occurring, did any American lab oversight or lab investigators ever appear in these labs to make sure they were safe labs?
12. Dr. Fauci, are you aware that Dr. Shi, who worked for the Wuhan Institute of Virology, was said — in her own words — to have over a hundred viruses that had not been previously published that could have been close enough relatives to create COVID‑19? Are you aware of that?
13. Did you fund gain‑of‑function research in other biolabs around the world?
14. (On the Fouchier avian‑flu work) Do you still stand by that? Would you like to qualify whether the knowledge of gain‑of‑function is worth the risks — do you think the risks really are worth the benefits for gain‑of‑function research?
15. Dr. Fauci, would you like to at least answer this one question and let us know whether you've changed your mind on whether the benefits of gain‑of‑function research outweigh the risks?
